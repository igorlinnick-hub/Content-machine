# YEDINO-ELEMENTS — what goes in the picture instead of glass blobs

Igor, 2026-10-05. Companion to `YEDINO-STYLE.md` (layout, tokens, frame furniture) and
`POST-CRAFT.md §5` (photo doctrine, Flux settings). Where they disagree on *what the image shows*,
this file wins for Yedino.

## Why this exists

R2 covers (`Yedino Templates/R2/contact-refs-vs-yedino.jpg`) read as generic SaaS: blue type on navy,
blue glass 3D objects, a Y app-icon. Nothing in them is about clinics, and nothing is warm. HireDrop's
carousels from the same reference set work because every slide carries something concrete and warm
(the Drop character) on a neutral page with violet only as a detail.

Yedino has no people to show: no doctor photos yet, and Igor is not the face of the brand.
So the warm, concrete thing is **the clinic's world** — what the clinics sell — **seen through the
production frame** — what Yedino does.

## The rule: clinic object × production frame

Every image = **one medical object** (stem cells, a peptide vial, an IV line, a PRP tube)
+ **one sign of filming** (viewfinder corners, a REC dot, a phone on a tripod, a teleprompter line,
a softbox). That pair is Yedino in one picture: content about medicine, made on set.

- **Blue lives only in the production layer** — corners, REC, teleprompter text, a phone UI, the
  progress rule. The object and the room are photographed warm (daylight, white, wood, steel).
  Page white or ink, blue as accent: the HireDrop proportion.
- **Separation from HWC:** HWC shows medicine directly (dark 3D renders, teal-amber grade). Yedino
  shows a 3D render **only inside a frame** — on a phone screen, a monitor, behind viewfinder corners.
  Never a bare full-bleed organ render on a Yedino slide.

## Element families

### A. Regenerative biology — renders, always inside a frame
Stem cells (translucent MSC cluster), exosomes, collagen fibres, cartilage surface, mitochondria
(NAD+), a peptide chain as a molecular model, a DNA helix, a receptor with a ligand.
*Use for:* "what clinics sell is hard to explain" → "we explain it on camera".
*Flux line:* `clean editorial 3D render of [subject], single subject, soft studio light, dark
neutral background, shown on a smartphone screen mounted on a tripod, shallow depth of field`.

### B. Clinic objects — still life, real photograph
Unlabelled peptide vials with lyophilised powder, amber dropper bottle, IV bag and drip chamber,
PRP tubes in a centrifuge, a blood-tube rack, a sterile tray with gauze, an ultrasound probe on its
cradle, a cold-chain shipping box, a capsule organiser.
*Use for:* service / offer posts, "your menu is the content".
*Flux line:* `real product photograph, [object], no labels, no text, morning daylight, white and
warm wood surfaces, calm, editorial, 4:5`.

### C. Production objects
Phone on a tripod, lavalier mic clipped to a white coat on a hanger, ring light, a tablet
teleprompter with a script, a clapperboard, an SD card, a gimbal, a light stand in a corner.
*Use for:* "how we work", "one visit → N videos", process slides.

### D. Hybrids — the signature, for covers
- A peptide vial on a seamless backdrop under a softbox, phone-on-tripod out of focus in front.
- A stem-cell render on a phone screen, framed by viewfinder corners and a REC dot.
- A teleprompter tablet whose script line is about NAD+ / peptides.
- A PRP centrifuge in an empty treatment room, a light stand and camera set up beside it.
- Four unlabelled vials in a row, each lit like a product shot = "one visit, four videos".

### E. Rooms, empty and set for filming
Treatment room in morning light, an IV lounge chair, a consult desk with two empty chairs — each with
one piece of gear in it (tripod, softbox, mic stand). *Use for:* "followers exist, where are the
consults?", visit / trust beats.

### F. Screens — the product itself
Content Machine: teleprompter, script card, publishing calendar, a clinic's Instagram grid,
analytics. Real screenshots, not mock-ups. *Use for:* proof slides, "what you get".

## Bans

- **No people, no faces, no hands with identifiable features** — AI people are banned project-wide
  (`POST-CRAFT §5`), and there are no doctor photos yet.
- **No labels or drug names on vials or bags.** Several peptides are on the FDA's restricted
  (category 2) list — a readable name on a bottle is a compliance problem, not a detail.
- No needle in skin, no blood drama, no before/after.
- No claims written into the image (no "heals", "regenerates", "anti-aging" on a bottle or screen).
- No glass blobs, abstract 3D shapes, gradients standing in for a subject, Y app-icon as a hero.
- One element per slide, and it must be the slide's actual subject (`POST-CRAFT §5` context rule).
